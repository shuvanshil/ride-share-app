Safety Standards & Guidelines

Your safety is our top priority on every single trip.

Safety Commitment

At LiphtUp, your safety is our top priority. We are committed to providing a secure, reliable, and respectful ride experience for every passenger and driver.

Our Safety Standards

  • Verified drivers with valid driving licences and required vehicle documents.
  • Vehicle and driver details displayed upfront before every ride.
  • Real-time trip tracking for complete journey transparency.
  • Live trip tracking link can be shared with up to 3 trusted emergency contacts.
  • Easy in-app reporting for unsafe behavior or incidents.
  • Zero tolerance policy for harassment, discrimination, violence, or fraud.
  • Personal information protected in strict compliance with our Privacy Policy.
  • Drivers expected to observe all traffic rules and drive responsibly.
  • Passengers and drivers must treat each other with mutual respect.
  • Emergency services should be alerted immediately in any life-threatening situation.

Passenger Guidelines

  • Verify the driver's name, photo, and vehicle number before boarding.
  • Do not carry illegal, hazardous, or prohibited items.
  • Report any suspicious or inappropriate behavior immediately through the app.
  • Treat your driver and their vehicle with care and respect.

Driver Guidelines

  • Keep your vehicle clean, safe, and roadworthy at all times.
  • Never drive under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or illegal substances.
  • Follow all speed limits, traffic signals, and safety laws strictly.
  • Do not request payments outside the official LiphtUp fare system.
  • Respect every passenger regardless of age, gender, religion, or background.

Building a Safe Community Together

LiphtUp continuously works to improve safety through secure technology, identity verification, and responsive customer support. Together, we build a trusted mobility network for everyone.
