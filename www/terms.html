Riders

Rider Terms & Conditions

  • Account Security: Users are solely responsible for keeping login credentials confidential and secure.
  • Accuracy of Information: Riders must provide accurate, current, and complete profile information.

  • Cancellation Policy: Standard cancellation guidelines apply if a ride is cancelled after driver acceptance or arrival.

4. Code of Conduct

  • Traffic Compliance: Riders must not ask drivers to violate traffic signals, speed limits, or safety laws.

Driver Terms & Conditions

1. Driver Eligibility & Documentation

  • Licensing: Drivers must possess a valid, non-expired driving licence for the corresponding vehicle category (Bike / Auto).
  • Vehicle Compliance: Vehicles must maintain active registration certificates, fitness, commercial permits, and updated insurance.
  • Verification: Drivers consent to identity, document, and background verification before activation.

2. Independent Contractor Relationship

  • Contractor Status: Drivers operate as independent service providers and are not employees, agents, or partners of LiphtUp.
  • Full Autonomy: Drivers retain full freedom to go online/offline and accept or decline trip requests at their discretion.

3. Fares & Earnings

  • Platform Fee: LiphtUp deducts standard platform service charges or fixed commissions from trip fares where applicable.
  • Payouts: Digital earnings collected on behalf of drivers are disbursed regularly according to local payout terms.

4. Operating Safety & Conduct

  • Safety Rules: Drivers must follow all traffic laws, speed limits, helmet/seatbelt regulations, and drive responsibly.
  • Zero Tolerance: Driving under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or engaging in unsafe driving leads to permanent suspension.
