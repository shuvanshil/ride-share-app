LiphtUp policy

Terms & Conditions

Rider Terms & Conditions

1. Account Registration & Eligibility

  • Age Requirement: Riders must be at least 18 years old to create an account.
  • Account Security: Users are solely responsible for maintaining password confidentiality.
  • Accuracy of Information: Riders must provide true, accurate, and updated personal profiles.

2. Services & Booking

  • Platform Role: The app acts purely as a digital intermediary connecting riders with independent third-party drivers.
  • Booking Rules: A booking is confirmed only when a driver accepts the request.
  • Usage Restrictions: Vehicles must not be used to transport illegal goods, hazardous materials, or excess luggage beyond capacity.

3. Fares, Payments & Cancellation

  • Fare Calculation: Fares are dynamic and based on distance, time, traffic, and peak hours.
  • Payment Methods: Riders agree to pay via linked credit/debit cards, digital wallets, or direct cash to the driver.
  • Cancellation Fee: Fees apply if a ride is cancelled after a specific time window or if the driver waits past the grace period.
  • Taxes & Tolls: Standard government taxes and route tolls are added to the final trip fare.

4. Code of Conduct

  • Behavior: Riders must treat drivers with respect. Verbal abuse, physical aggression, or harassment results in immediate bans.
  • Vehicle Care: Damaging or fouling the vehicle will incur cleaning or repair charges.
  • Safety: Seatbelts, if available, must be worn, and riders must not force drivers to violate traffic laws.

Driver Terms & Conditions

1. Driver Eligibility & Documentation

  • Licensing: Drivers must hold a valid commercial driving licence for 3-wheelers.
  • Vehicle Requirements: The auto-rickshaw must have valid registration, fitness certificates, and updated commercial insurance.
  • Background Verification: Drivers consent to mandatory criminal record and driving history background checks.

2. Nature of Relationship

  • Independent Contractor: Drivers are independent service providers. They are not employees, agents, or partners of the app.
  • Flexibility: Drivers maintain full autonomy to log on/off the app and accept or decline trip requests at will.

3. Fares, Commission & Payouts

  • Platform Fee: The app deducts a predefined percentage, or commission, from each completed trip fare.
  • Payout Schedule: Driver earnings from digital payments are processed and transferred on a weekly or daily schedule.
  • Tipping: Drivers retain 100% of any voluntary tips provided by riders.

4. Operating Rules & Conduct

  • Safety & Compliance: Drivers must strictly follow all regional traffic rules, speed limits, and transport guidelines.
  • Route Choice: Drivers must follow the app's GPS navigation unless the rider requests a specific alternative.
  • Device Usage: Mobile devices must be mounted safely. Texting or calling while driving is strictly prohibited.
  • Discrimination: Zero-tolerance policy for discrimination based on race, religion, gender, nationality, or disability.

Mutual Clauses

1. Data Privacy & Location Tracking

  • GPS Tracking: Real-time location tracking is active during trips for safety and fare validation.
  • Data Privacy: Personal data handling strictly follows the platform's standard Privacy Policy.

2. Limitation of Liability

  • Service Interruptions: The platform is provided "as-is" and is not liable for app downtime or GPS inaccuracies.
  • Third-Party Actions: The company is not liable for any property damage, injury, or loss caused by the actions of riders or drivers.

3. Termination & Suspension

  • Account Deactivation: The platform reserves the right to suspend accounts instantly for fraud, low ratings, or policy violations.
