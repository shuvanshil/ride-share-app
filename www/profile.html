Safety

At LiphtUp, your safety is our top priority. We are committed to providing a secure, reliable, and respectful ride experience for every passenger and driver.

Our Safety Standards

  • Verified drivers with valid driving licences and required documents.
  • Vehicle information is displayed before every ride.
  • Real-time trip tracking for transparency.
  • Trip details can be shared with trusted contacts.
  • Easy reporting of unsafe behavior or incidents.
  • Zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, violence, or fraud.
  • Personal information is protected in accordance with our Privacy Policy.
  • Drivers are expected to follow all traffic laws and drive responsibly.
  • Passengers and drivers must treat each other with respect.
  • Emergency assistance should be contacted immediately in case of any life-threatening situation.

Passenger Guidelines

  • Verify the driver's name, photo, and vehicle number before boarding.
  • Do not carry illegal or hazardous items.
  • Report any suspicious or inappropriate behavior through the app.

Driver Guidelines

  • Keep your vehicle clean and roadworthy.
  • Never drive under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  • Follow all traffic rules and speed limits.
  • Do not request payments outside the official LiphtUp fare system.
  • Respect every passenger regardless of age, gender, religion, or background.

Our Commitment

LiphtUp continuously works to improve safety through secure technology, identity verification, and responsive customer support. Together, we can build a trusted transportation community for everyone.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I book a ride?

Open the LiphtUp app, enter your pickup and destination locations, choose your preferred ride option, and confirm your booking.

2. How can I cancel a ride?

You can cancel a ride before it begins through the app. Cancellation charges may apply based on our cancellation policy.

3. How do I contact my driver?

Once your ride is confirmed, you can use the contact option available in the app to communicate with your driver.

4. What should I do if I leave an item in the vehicle?

Report the issue through the app or contact our support team as soon as possible. We will do our best to help you reconnect with the driver.

5. How do I report a safety concern?

If you experience any unsafe behavior or encounter an emergency, use the in-app reporting feature or contact our support team immediately. If there is an immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.

6. How do I update my account information?

You can update your profile details from the account settings section of the LiphtUp app.

7. Need More Help?

LiphtUp Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 8, 2026

At LiphtUp, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data when you use our services.

Information We Collect

We may collect:

  • Full name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • Profile photo, if provided
  • Location data during rides
  • Ride history
  • Payment information, where applicable
  • Device and app usage information

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Connect passengers with drivers.
  • Process ride bookings and payments.
  • Improve the quality and security of our services.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Send important notifications and service updates.
  • Prevent fraud and ensure platform safety.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

Location Information

Location data is used only to provide ride booking, driver matching, navigation, trip tracking, and related safety features. We do not use your location for unrelated purposes without your permission.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to help protect your personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only:

  • With the assigned driver or passenger to complete a ride.
  • With trusted service providers that help operate our platform.
  • When required by law or a valid legal request.
  • To protect the safety, rights, or property of LiphtUp, our users, or others.

Your Rights

You may:

  • Access your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your account, subject to legal obligations.
  • Contact us with privacy-related questions.

Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our policies.

Children's Privacy

LiphtUp is not intended for children below the minimum age permitted by applicable law to use our services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published within the app or on our official website with the updated effective date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the LiphtUp support team through our official app or website.

LiphtUp policy

Terms & Conditions

Rider Terms & Conditions

1. Account Registration & Eligibility

  • Age Requirement: Riders must be at least 18 years old to create an account.
  • Account Security: Users are solely responsible for maintaining password confidentiality.
  • Accuracy of Information: Riders must provide true, accurate, and updated personal profiles.

2. Services & Booking

  • Platform Role: The app acts purely as a digital intermediary connecting riders with independent third-party drivers.
  • Booking Rules: A booking is confirmed only when a driver accepts the request.
  • Usage Restrictions: Vehicles must not be used to transport illegal goods, hazardous materials, or excess luggage beyond capacity.

3. Fares, Payments & Cancellation

  • Fare Calculation: Fares are dynamic and based on distance, time, traffic, and peak hours.
  • Payment Methods: Riders agree to pay via linked credit/debit cards, digital wallets, or direct cash to the driver.
  • Cancellation Fee: Fees apply if a ride is cancelled after a specific time window or if the driver waits past the grace period.
  • Taxes & Tolls: Standard government taxes and route tolls are added to the final trip fare.

4. Code of Conduct

  • Behavior: Riders must treat drivers with respect. Verbal abuse, physical aggression, or harassment results in immediate bans.
  • Vehicle Care: Damaging or fouling the vehicle will incur cleaning or repair charges.
  • Safety: Seatbelts, if available, must be worn, and riders must not force drivers to violate traffic laws.

Driver Terms & Conditions

1. Driver Eligibility & Documentation

  • Licensing: Drivers must hold a valid commercial driving licence for 3-wheelers.
  • Vehicle Requirements: The auto-rickshaw must have valid registration, fitness certificates, and updated commercial insurance.
  • Background Verification: Drivers consent to mandatory criminal record and driving history background checks.

2. Nature of Relationship

  • Independent Contractor: Drivers are independent service providers. They are not employees, agents, or partners of the app.
  • Flexibility: Drivers maintain full autonomy to log on/off the app and accept or decline trip requests at will.

3. Fares, Commission & Payouts

  • Platform Fee: The app deducts a predefined percentage, or commission, from each completed trip fare.
  • Payout Schedule: Driver earnings from digital payments are processed and transferred on a weekly or daily schedule.
  • Tipping: Drivers retain 100% of any voluntary tips provided by riders.

4. Operating Rules & Conduct

  • Safety & Compliance: Drivers must strictly follow all regional traffic rules, speed limits, and transport guidelines.
  • Route Choice: Drivers must follow the app's GPS navigation unless the rider requests a specific alternative.
  • Device Usage: Mobile devices must be mounted safely. Texting or calling while driving is strictly prohibited.
  • Discrimination: Zero-tolerance policy for discrimination based on race, religion, gender, nationality, or disability.

Mutual Clauses

1. Data Privacy & Location Tracking

  • GPS Tracking: Real-time location tracking is active during trips for safety and fare validation.
  • Data Privacy: Personal data handling strictly follows the platform's standard Privacy Policy.

2. Limitation of Liability

  • Service Interruptions: The platform is provided "as-is" and is not liable for app downtime or GPS inaccuracies.
  • Third-Party Actions: The company is not liable for any property damage, injury, or loss caused by the actions of riders or drivers.

3. Termination & Suspension

  • Account Deactivation: The platform reserves the right to suspend accounts instantly for fraud, low ratings, or policy violations.
