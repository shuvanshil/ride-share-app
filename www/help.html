Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I book a ride?
Open the LiphtUp app, enter your pickup location and destination, select your preferred ride option (Bike or Auto), check the fare estimate, and tap Book Now.
2. How can I cancel a ride?
You can cancel a ride before it starts through the active trip screen in the app. Please note that cancellation charges may apply if cancelled after a specific grace period.
3. How do I contact my assigned driver?
Once your booking is accepted by a driver, you will see call and message buttons on the trip tracking screen to communicate directly with your driver.
4. What should I do if I leave an item in the vehicle?
Contact our support team immediately with your ride details (date, time, pickup/destination). We will help connect you with your driver to retrieve your lost item.
5. How do I report a safety concern?
You can report safety concerns directly from the Safety section in your Profile menu or from your active ride screen. In any immediate emergency, contact local emergency services (112 / 100) first.
6. How do I update my account information?
Navigate to the Profile tab in the app, tap the Edit Profile button, update your details (name, email, photo), and save changes.
